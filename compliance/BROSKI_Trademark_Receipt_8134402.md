## 2. Legal Rights Unlocked

1. **™ Symbol Authorization:**
   - Under Indian intellectual property practice, having a formal TM-A filing and application number (`8134402`) gives the proprietor the immediate right to display the **™** symbol (`BROSKI™`) on all packaging, digital properties, social media, invoices, and advertising.
2. **® Symbol Restriction:**
   - Under Section 107 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999, the **®** (registered) symbol cannot be used until the Registry completes examination, publishes the mark in the Trade Marks Journal (with no opposition), and issues the official Certificate of Registration.
