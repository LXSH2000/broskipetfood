## 2. Technical Print Specifications (Rohan / Packaging)

1. **Sticker Size:** 105 mm (width) × 148 mm (height) — A6 portrait sticker, fits neatly on the back of the 400g pouch.
2. **Font Height Standards (Second Schedule):**
   - For package net quantity > 200 g and ≤ 500 g:
   - **Net Quantity numeral (`400 g`):** Minimum numeral height **4.0 mm**.
   - **MRP numeral (`₹149.00` / `₹179.00`):** Minimum numeral height **3.0 mm**.
   - **All other text:** Minimum font height **1.5 mm** for clear legibility.
3. **Finish:** Matte-laminated, water-resistant self-adhesive paper/PP sticker so print doesn't smudge if pet parents handle with damp hands.
