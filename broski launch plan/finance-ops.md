# Broski: finance and operations plan

Planning date: 30 September 2026. All figures INR. These are auditable planning assumptions, not supplier quotations, proven demand or a forecast. Launch one outsourced 200 g SKU: 500 sealed retail packs, not a factory. Founder availability assumed 10 hours/week. The working website is not yet live commerce.

All allocations are gross cash caps including applicable supplier taxes. Do not assume input-tax-credit refunds fund the launch. If quotations exceed a cap, reduce scope or defer the pilot; never silently increase the budget.

## Offers and contribution

Candidate prices: single ₹249 plus ₹59 delivery; two-pack ₹449 delivered; three-pack ₹649 delivered. No extra discount is modeled. Delivery applies only to quoted serviceable lanes; do not promise pan-India flat-rate coverage until packed weights/dimensions and representative routes are priced. Two packs contain 400 g of product before packaging and may cross the 500 g billable slab; three packs exceed 500 g before packaging.

**Tax scenario, not tax advice:** provisionally assume registered seller, 18% output GST included in the entire customer collection including delivery, and no usable input tax credit. Classification, registration, shipping tax treatment and invoicing must be confirmed by the separate compliance review before prices go live. Gateway fee GST is distinct from product GST. Manufacturer ₹85 is treated as gross landed cost even if input credits later prove available.

Razorpay's published standard domestic planning rate is 2% plus 18% GST on its fee, modeled as 2.36% of gross collection. Do not rely on introductory zero-fee promotions. [Official pricing](https://razorpay.com/pricing/) and [fee explanation](https://razorpay.com/blog/razorpay-payment-gateway-pricing-explained/).

Courier assumptions ₹65/₹75/₹85 per single/two/three-pack order are **gross planning placeholders, not Shiprocket quotes**. Courier charges depend on weight, dimensions, pickup/delivery location and carrier. Obtain actual serviceability/rate results including taxes and surcharges before accepting an order. [Shiprocket calculator](https://www.shiprocket.in/shipping-rate-calculator/) and [official FAQ](https://www.shiprocket.in/faq/).

Use the three-pack as the paid-acquisition candidate. Single-pack paid advertising at ₹80 CAC loses money in this model. ## Three illustrative 90-day cases

Period starts with project kickoff, not the first sale. A conditional 6–8-week preparation period leaves only approximately 5–7 selling weeks. Volumes are demand scenarios, not promises. All orders are prepaid, delivered and settled within the illustration; reserve tax amounts immediately. Assume 20% single, 50% two-pack, 30% three-pack orders in each case: 2.1 packs/order and ₹480.80 gross average collection. Acquisition cost here is cash campaign spend divided by **all** orders (blended cost); do not present it as measured new-customer CAC if repeats occur.

Reserve 50 of the 500 packs for retained samples, evaluation, sampling and replacement stock; at most 450 are initially saleable. For a conservative profit illustration expense all 50 reserve packs at ₹4,250, whether or not consumed; final accounting must distinguish retained inventory from consumed samples. No reorder or loan is included.

Cash formula: ₹2,00,000 − ₹77,000 setup/ops − ₹42,500 entire stock purchase + net-of-tax collections − actual order-level courier/outer pack/gateway/loss allowance − acquisition. Sold inventory is **not deducted again** in this cash formula. Remaining cash includes the ₹30,000 contingency and unspent working-capital allowances; it is not profit or freely distributable owner income. Loss/tax amounts are treated as ring-fenced even before payment. No receivable settlement delay or supplier credit is assumed. Unsold stock is an asset only to the extent saleable before expiry; write-offs worsen the result.

In the strong case courier ₹15,200, outer packs ₹3,600, gateway approximately ₹2,269 and loss allowance ₹3,000 all stay within their respective caps. All cases stay within the initial batch. ₹77,000 overhead recovery at working-case ₹28.61 contribution/order would need about 2,692 orders before reserve-pack cost or owner time: the first 500-pack batch is a validation exercise, not full startup-cost payback.

Founder time is unpaid in cash figures. At a hypothetical ₹500/hour and 130 hours over 13 weeks, imputed owner labour is ₹65,000 additional economic cost. This is an assumption, not a wage quotation. Do not call the operating figures take-home profit.

## Conditional release gates and operations

1. **Weeks 1–2, cap ₹12,000 released:** interview target dog owners, shortlist at least three manufacturers, obtain comparable landed RFQs for 500 packs, verify MOQ, traceability, sealed packaging, batch coding and ingredient disclosure. No paid preorder until production/quality obligations are understood. Keep a supplier fallback.
2. **Weeks 2–4, cap additional ₹37,000:** obtain professional formulation/label/classification input and the laboratory/QA scope. These are budget limits, not assurances of sufficient tests. Reject the launch if credible testing/shelf-life requirements exceed available time or money; reallocate within the hard cap or pause. No unsupported nutrition or health claims.
3. **Weeks 4–6, cap additional ₹60,500:** approve recipe/label and written acceptance criteria, then release ₹42,500 manufacturing plus ₹10,000 artwork/photos and ₹8,000 storefront/provider work. Negotiate deposit/balance against accepted stock rather than assuming supplier credit. All upfront spending remains inside allocation caps.
4. **Weeks 6–8, conditional release:** receive stock, reconcile units and lot codes, retain samples, check seals/weights and release only QA-approved stock. Run provider sandbox and controlled paid-order/refund/dispatch checks. Confirm actual shipping for one/two/three packs across target PIN codes. Start narrowly; failed QA moves the date.
5. **Weeks 8–13:** outsource scheduled packing/dispatch; founder handles exceptions and metrics. Release ₹10,000 help budget against a clear job scope and ₹35,500 variable float only as orders need it. Initial ad tranche ≤₹3,000 from the ₹15,000 cap; review delivered-order economics before the next tranche. Small samples are noisy—do not extrapolate ROAS from a handful of sales.

Weekly founder 10-hour allocation: supplier/QA 2h, acquisition/customer interviews 3h, content/offer approval 1h, cash/tax reconciliation 1h, customer exceptions 1h, contractor coordination/dispatch audit 1h, buffer 1h. Outsourced helper packs/labels orders and performs routine support within a negotiated scope; accountant and manufacturer handle their specialist work. At 200 orders in the remaining 5–7 selling weeks, dispatch is approximately 29–40 orders/week; validate helper time/cost before promising next-day handling. Owner availability is not a claim that all tasks fit without outsourcing.

Maintain lot-level stock records, first-expiry-first-out picking, supplier invoices, complaint/return log and a batch withdrawal contact procedure. Keep customer data access limited to fulfilment needs. Founder checks collections against gateway settlements and courier invoices weekly, separates tax reserves, and keeps batch/supplier evidence available.

**Reorder gate:** reorder only after actual landed and delivery costs are known, usable stock is reconciled, repeat purchase evidence exists, no unresolved quality complaints remain, and a written cash forecast protects tax/refund obligations plus the ₹30,000 reserve. A second 500-pack batch at the same hypothetical rate costs another ₹42,500; this is not included in scenario sales and is not authorized merely because gross collections look healthy. Use net available cash after obligations, reduce batch size if offered, or stop at pilot completion.
