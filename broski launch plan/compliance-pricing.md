# Broski India: compliance and competitor pricing research

Prepared 1 October 2026. Scope: domestically outsourced pilot of one or two dog-biscuit/treat SKUs within a total INR 200,000 launch budget. Primary-source desk research; outstanding applicability questions are launch gates, not permission to ignore requirements. No supplier was contacted. Workflow: agency-legal-compliance-checker.

## What is established, and what must be confirmed

| Topic | Evidence and practical implication | Before spending on production |
|---|---|---|
| FSSAI | The FSS Act definition of food excludes animal feed. A human-food FSSAI licence is therefore not, by itself, proof that a pet-treat operation has the necessary permissions. | Obtain a written, state-specific checklist for the actual manufacturer and Broski's activities from a qualified local adviser/relevant authorities. Check applicable trade, factory, pollution-control and animal-feed requirements; do not advertise “FSSAI approved pet treats” merely because a supplier holds a food licence. |
| BIS | The BIS committee programme lists **IS 11968:2019, Pet food for dogs and cats — Specification**, reviewed in 2024. This review did not establish a current pet-treat-specific mandatory certification order. | Ask BIS/manufacturer to confirm the standard's scope for complementary biscuits versus complete feed, and current mandatory-certification applicability. Do not print a BIS mark without an applicable valid licence. Do not state either “BIS mandatory” or “no BIS requirements” as a settled finding. |
| Retail packaging | Legal Metrology packaged-commodity requirements and manufacturer/packer registration need assessment. Core artwork checks include responsible manufacturer/packer identity and address, common/generic product name, net quantity, MRP inclusive of taxes, applicable date declaration, consumer-care contacts, and applicable unit-sale-price declaration. | Have the state Legal Metrology controller/adviser validate current declarations, exemptions and who must register under Rule 27 for the outsourced arrangement. Approve the exact printed artwork before ordering packaging. |
| GST | CBIC's published rates FAQ explicitly places dog/cat food under heading **2309 at 18%**. However, that FAQ contains historical schedule references; the latest amended rate notification was not fully reconciled in this review. | Model 18% provisionally, then obtain written CA confirmation of current rate, detailed HSN, registration and invoicing treatment before selling. State, interstate and e-commerce circumstances matter; do not assume a blanket small-business exemption. |
| Plastic EPR | CPCB's FAQ distinguishes producers, importers and brand owners; it describes a micro/small-brand-owner exemption from EPR obligations. This is not a blanket exemption for every MSME or every role. | Confirm Broski's precise role and current registration/obligation treatment with CPCB/SPCB, including any recent amendments. Keep applicable Udyam evidence, pouch-material specifications, packaging weights and supplier invoices. A pouch supplier's certificate does not automatically resolve Broski's obligations. |
| Trademark | IP India's official fee schedule lists INR 4,500 electronic filing for eligible individuals/startups/small enterprises. | Reserve at least INR 4,500 per mark per class plus any professional/search costs. Search “Broski” and similar marks, confirm the appropriate class, and clear packaging before printing. Filing is not registration; brand availability has not been verified. |

## Recommended procurement and safety gates

These are commercial quality controls, not a claim that each is an independently mandatory statutory test. Require the manufacturer's legal identity, facility address, applicable permissions, references, signed product specification, ingredient/allergen disclosure, batch traceability and complaint/recall procedure. Have a qualified veterinary nutrition professional review suitability and feeding directions. Use complementary-treat positioning unless complete-feed adequacy has actually been substantiated.

Agree testing with a laboratory accredited for the relevant methods: ingredient-appropriate microbiological, moisture/water-activity, rancidity/oxidation and contaminant testing as advised. Obtain evidence supporting the proposed shelf life in the final pouch under intended storage conditions; do not copy a competitor's shelf life. Retain batch samples and certificates of analysis. Require written approval before recipe, ingredient supplier or packaging changes. An attractive pouch and “natural” wording are not evidence of safety.

Artwork should also carry useful batch/lot identification, supported best-before/storage instructions, ingredient information, feeding guidance and a clear statement that this is a dog treat. Avoid unsupported dental, disease, hypoallergenic, “vet approved,” organic, human-grade or environmental claims. These are recommended claim-control principles; obtain a final legal artwork check for the chosen formula and claims.

Use a small run of suitable stock pouches with durable approved labels until compliance, seal integrity and demand are proven. Hold production spend until the above gates are resolved. Supplier quotations, professional fees, laboratory scope and minimum order quantities remain unverified.

Research accessed 30 September–1 October 2026. Prices are displayed tax-inclusive prices before shipping, not wholesale quotations. Pages may change; stock status is not a delivery commitment. These products differ in formula and positioning, so comparisons are price context rather than nutritional equivalence.

| Dogsee, Gigabites Carrot Cookies | 300 g | 199 | 66.33 | [Official product page](https://dogseechew.in/product/dogsee-gigabites-carrot-cookies-for-dogs). Page displayed no stock during review. |
| Mother of Strays, All New Dog Biscuits | 400 g entry variant | 299 | 74.75 | [Official product page](https://motherofstrays.in/products/chicken-biscuits). Displayed INR 299, smallest option 400 g; page displayed sold out. Treat this as an indicative entry-variant benchmark, recheck selected variant before a published like-for-like price claim. |

The earlier Broski website concept of INR 349 for 200 g equals INR 174.50/100 g, approximately 17% above the HUFT benchmark. That is a premium hypothesis, not evidence of willingness to pay. Test paid demand and repeat purchase before locking it into printed MRP or committing to a second SKU. Do not compete only on the lowest price: product consistency, a clear ingredient explanation, suitable pack size and reliable service must justify any premium.

If GST-registered and the confirmed rate is 18%, a tax-inclusive selling price of INR 349 represents approximately INR 295.76 ex-GST revenue before payment fees, shipping, discounts, returns, product and packaging costs. Recoverable input tax credits depend on the business's actual eligibility and documentation. An unregistered business must not separately collect GST. The main launch model should state its GST assumptions explicitly and have the CA validate them.

## Items still unresolved

Manufacturer's location and approvals; final recipe and label; BIS applicability/QCO status; current GST notification reconciliation and registration treatment; EPR role/exemption/registration; Broski trademark availability; testing and shelf-life evidence; supplier MOQ/cost. Resolve these before taking paid orders or dispatching product. No claim in this note represents a completed registration, clearance, laboratory test or supplier agreement.
