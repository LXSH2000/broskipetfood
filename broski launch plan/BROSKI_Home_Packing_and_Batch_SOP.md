## 1. Packing Station Setup & Hygiene Protocol

1. **Room Preparation:**
   - Dedicated clean table surface disinfected with food-grade surface sanitizing wipes.
   - Closed windows / doors to avoid dust from outside street.
   - Clean, dry stainless steel / food-grade plastic scoops.
2. **Personal Hygiene:**
   - Hand washing with soap for 30 seconds followed by hand sanitizer.
   - Disposable food-grade nitrile gloves (changed every 2 hours).
   - Clean hairnet and apron. No loose jewelry or watches.
3. **Materials Ready:**
   - Empty 400g stand-up zipper pouches (Yellow for Non-Veg, White for Mix).
   - Pre-printed back compliance stickers + front brand stickers.
   - Hand impulse heat sealer (set to temperature setting 3.5 – 4.0 for aluminum/foil-backed pouches).
   - Legal Metrology stamped digital bench scale.

## 2. Scale Calibration & Legal Metrology Weight Tolerances

Under Legal Metrology Rules, 2011 (Maximum Permissible Error / MPE for 400 g is **3% = 12 g**):
- **Gross Target Weight:**
  - Net biscuit weight target: **402 g – 404 g** (always pack +2g to +4g safety buffer to guarantee zero underweight packs).
  - Pouch + sticker tare weight: **~15 g**.
  - Target scale reading with pouch: **417 g – 419 g**.
- **Tolerance Limits:**
  - **Absolute Minimum Net Weight:** **388 g** (any pack under 388g is legally defective and MUST be rejected/re-filled).
  - **Batch Average:** Must be ≥ **400.0 g**.

### Weighing Step-by-Step
1. Turn on the stamped digital scale on a level, vibration-free surface. Ensure it reads `0.000 kg`.
2. Place empty pouch on scale → Press `TARE` (scale reads `0.000 kg`).
3. Scoop biscuits into pouch until the scale displays between **402 g and 405 g**.
4. Check through the front clear window: ensure whole biscuits are displayed without dust or broken crumbs obstructing the window view.

---

## 3. Heat Sealing & Quality Inspection

1. **Air Evacuation:** Gently press pouch from the bottom upwards to remove excess air, then close the ziplock track firmly.
2. **Heat Seal:**
   - Align the top edge of the pouch into the impulse heat sealer (above the tear notches).
   - Press the sealing arm down until the red indicator light turns off + hold for 1 extra second for clean cooling.
   - Inspect the seal: Must be a solid, continuous straight band without bubbles, wrinkles, or channels.
3. **Drop / Leak Test:** Lightly squeeze the sealed pouch. If air hisses or escapes, discard pouch and re-seal.
4. **Sticker Application:** Apply front logo sticker centered above the window; apply back compliance sticker straight and centered on the reverse.
