## 1. Commercial Terms & Pitch Deck for Shopkeepers

### The Math for the Shopkeeper
- **Non-Veg 400 g MRP:** ₹149 (₹0.37/g)
  - Shopkeeper Purchase Price (30% margin): **₹104.30** per pack
  - Profit per pack sold: **₹44.70**
  - **Launch Offer (11 + 1 Free):**
    - Buy 11 packs for ₹1,147.30 → Get 12 packs total
    - Effective cost per pack: **₹95.60**
    - Total profit on 12 packs: ₹1,788 - ₹1,147.30 = **₹640.70 (35.8% margin)**
- **Mix 400 g MRP:** ₹179 (₹0.45/g)
  - Shopkeeper Purchase Price (30% margin): **₹125.30** per pack
  - Profit per pack sold: **₹53.70**
  - **Launch Offer (11 + 1 Free):**
    - Buy 11 packs for ₹1,378.30 → Get 12 packs total
    - Effective cost per pack: **₹114.86**
    - Total profit on 12 packs: ₹2,148 - ₹1,378.30 = **₹769.70 (35.8% margin)**

## 2. 60-Second Hinglish Pitch Script for Lakshay

> *"Namaste bhaiya! Mera naam Lakshay hai, Nangloi se hoon. Humne West Delhi ke pet parents ke liye ek fresh local dog biscuit brand shuru kiya hai — **BROSKI**.*  
>  
> *Bhaiya, market mein Pedigree ya Purepet pe aapko sirf 18–20% margin milta hai aur product 6 mahine purana hota hai. Humare 400g pouch mein dog parents ko bada clear window milta hai jisse unhe biscuit ki fresh baking aur crunch direct dikhta hai.*  
>  
> *Aapke liye offer ekdum simple hai:*  
> *1. **30% flat margin** (Pedigree se lagbhag double).*  
> *2. **Launch offer: 11 pack pe 1 pack free** (effective margin 36%).*  
> *3. Hum 1 box (6 Non-Veg + 6 Mix) ka small test order de rahe hain. 15 din mein agar ek bhi packet na bika, toh bina kisi question ke hum wapas utha lenge aur cash refund karenge.*  
>  
> *Aap counter pe yeh ek sample pack rakhiye, dog parents dekhte hi try karenge. Ek box se start karein bhaiya?"*

### Top 3 Objections & Answers
1. **"Naya brand hai, bikega nahi":**  
   *“Bhaiya isliye 11+1 launch offer de rahe hain aur 100% buy-back guarantee hai. Agar 15 din mein nahi nikla, main khud aakar stock wapas le jaunga. Aapka ₹1 ka bhi risk nahi hai.”*
2. **"Shop me jagah nahi hai":**  
   *“Bhaiya yeh stand-up zipper pouch hai, counter pe sirf 8 inch ki jagah leta hai. Window se biscuit dikhta hai toh customer khud poochta hai.”*
3. **"Credit pe doge?":**  
   *“Pehla test order cash on delivery / UPI rahega bhaiya. Jaise hi reorder aayega, hum aapko 15 days ka credit cycle set kar denge.”*

## 4. Sales Rules (Founder's Guardrails)
1. **Never drop less than 6 packs** (e.g. 3 Non-Veg + 3 Mix minimum trial box).
2. **First order is always Cash on Delivery / Instant UPI**.
3. **No shop gets stock with less than 4 months shelf life** (batch date must be fresh).
4. **Log every visit in the Sales Diary**: Store Name, Owner Name, Contact Number, Outcome (Took 12 packs / Took 6 packs / Follow-up date / Refused reason).
