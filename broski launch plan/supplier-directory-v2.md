5. **Mannu-G:** verification-first biscuit lead, not a deposit-ready alternative. Furry Feast and Al Moin are adjacent treat fallbacks requiring a different product/economics decision.

Pilot landed-cost target **₹75–95 per accepted 200 g pack** remains an assumption, not any supplier's quote. At ₹85, 500 packs consume ₹42,500. This must include product, pouch, labels, factory packing/sealing/coding, master cartons, inward freight and non-recoverable tax. One-time samples, specialist work, testing and individual-order shipping are separate. The ₹22,000 QA/testing budget in the master model is also unquoted. Do not spend the ₹30,000 contingency to conceal an uneconomic MOQ or inadequate safety evidence.

## Packaging brief: send identical requirements to each bidder

**Primary pack:** one 200 g SKU, factory-filled opaque or appropriately metallized stand-up pouch, reclosure zipper plus intact top heat seal, tear notches if compatible, external front/back labels, legible batch/date coding. Pouch dimensions remain provisional until actual-fill tests. Request layer-by-layer laminate, total thickness/tolerances, food-contact declaration/migration evidence appropriate to intended use, oxygen and moisture barrier values with test conditions, seal temperature/dwell/pressure range, storage conditions, traceability and declared shelf life of empty packaging. The food technologist sets necessary OTR/WVTR performance; this directory does not invent universal numerical limits. A silver appearance, “food-grade” claim or thicker film alone is insufficient.

**Labels:** request two sizes based on approved pouch dieline, 500 labels of each design plus agreed waste/proof quantity, opaque legible print, adhesive that survives expected heat/humidity/handling on the actual pouch, no ink transfer, scuff-resistant finish. Leave seal and coding windows clear. Obtain artwork proof with dimensions and material code. Do not print health claims, certifications or recycling claims without evidence. Final regulatory label review precedes the full print order.

**Outer packaging:** price separate one-pack and three-pack customer-shipping boxes plus factory master cartons. Supply filled pouch dimensions and weights; ask vendor to recommend board/flute/strength against the courier route. Specify internal dimensions, cushioning, void space, closure tape and maximum stacking loads. Dimensional-weight charges matter as much as box unit cost. Master cartons within manufacturing landed cost must not be charged again as customer-order cartons.

**Sample sequence:** request approximately 10–20 empty pouches in each of two or three sizes, subject to vendor sample terms; fill at the factory with actual product; assess fit, seal clearance and 200 g net fill; apply physical label proofs; have factory/lab verify seal integrity and agreed barrier evidence; conduct realistic courier/drop/handling trials with filled boxes, documenting crumbs and pack failures. These commercial fit trials do not establish safety or shelf life. Only then buy the factory-agreed packaging quantity. If pouches sell only in lots of 100, a requirement of 550 means purchasing 600, not assuming a 550-piece order is available.

## Ten-minute screening call: script and disqualifiers

Open: “We are evaluating Broski's first Indian domestic batch: one reward-biscuit SKU, 500 accepted 200 g packs, existing validated recipe, factory packing. This is an enquiry, not an order.”

1. Are you the manufacturer or trader? What legal entity, GST details, plant and bank beneficiary will appear on the contract? Do you supply domestic startup brands?
2. What is the minimum per recipe, pack size and artwork? Is 500 accepted finished packs possible? What exact output covers losses, retained samples and legal fill tolerances?
3. What existing recipe/pack combination has transferable stability evidence? Who signs formulation/use/feeding guidance? What changes would invalidate evidence?
4. Which process controls, ingredient/allergen records, batch COA and traceability documents can be supplied? Can our specialist review them under confidentiality?
5. Can you procure the approved stock pouch/labels and seal/code at the plant? Whose liability covers sealing and packaging defects?
6. What is included in the per-pack price, one-time fees, tax, inward freight and testing? Are we being quoted bulk kilograms or accepted units?
7. What samples, lead times, quote validity, deposit milestones and defect/replacement terms apply? Ask for dates, not “fast delivery.”
8. Can finished stock remain on hold pending acceptance? How are deviations, contamination complaints, withdrawals and recall costs handled?

Stop commercial progression for refusal to identify the factory/legal entity, unsupported shelf-life promises, unexplained bank-name mismatch, refusal of traceability or pressure to pay before specification/QA agreement. A high MOQ is a commercial mismatch, not proof of supplier misconduct.

## Quote comparison and final selection

Keep one row per supplier: legal entity; direct/adjacent fit; MOQ; accepted quantity; net fill; product cost; primary-pack/label cost; factory packing; master carton; freight; tax treatment; one-time fees; evidence gaps; quote date/validity; deposit; dispatch date; payment beneficiary; remedies. Calculate landed recurring cost divided by **accepted retail packs**, not kilograms or all output including rejects. Only after mandatory safety/legal/traceability gates pass, score documented quality/evidence 35%, landed economics 25%, MOQ/cash exposure 20%, delivery 10%, support/remedy terms 10%. Low price cannot offset a failed gate.

Founder execution within 10 hours/week: week 1 collect three comparable manufacturing/packaging responses and one lab scope; week 2 shortlist two viable paths and commission approved samples; week 3 compare samples/evidence and resolve quote gaps. Production calendar begins only after formulation, pack, label, contract and QA gates pass. A 500-pack minimum with 50 packs reserved means at most 450 packs initially available for sale; expert-required samples/retains may change that allocation. Record council decision: keep one SKU and the contingency; change quantity/schedule before compromising evidence or making an unquoted commitment.
