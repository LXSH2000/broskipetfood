# Broski brand direction

**Name:** Broski  
**Tagline:** Treats for your best bro.

Broski is a playful dog biscuit storefront concept built around the everyday bond between a dog and their person. The voice is warm, direct and affectionate, with short phrases and a little mischief.

## Visual direction

Use warm cream backgrounds, deep brown typography and biscuit-toned details. Handwritten display lettering gives the identity its personality; readable supporting type carries product information and checkout instructions. Keep generous spacing and clear contrast. Product imagery and movement should support choosing treats, with reduced-motion alternatives available.

Use the same Broski spelling in navigation, packaging concepts, page titles and confirmations. Keep the tagline verbatim. Ingredient and care information should be plain and precise rather than playful where clarity matters.

## Copy principles

- Lead with the product and the moment it is for.
- Describe only ingredients, processes and benefits that the owner can substantiate.
- Avoid invented customer reviews, awards, certifications, veterinarian endorsements or sales figures.
- Distinguish preview transactions from actual purchases wherever checkout is shown.

The name is a working identity, not a cleared trademark. Name, domain and handle availability have not been checked. Generated imagery is concept artwork unless replaced or explicitly verified against real products.
